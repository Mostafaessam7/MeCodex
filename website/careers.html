Careers

A small, senior-led studio — no bench, no bureaucracy.

MeCodex stays intentionally lean: experienced engineers working directly on client problems, not layers of management between the work and the people doing it. Here's how we work, what we're hiring for right now, and what we look for even when a specific role isn't open.

Remote-first, by design

We're based out of Cairo, Egypt, and work remote-first with clients and collaborators wherever they are. What matters is overlap for weekly demos and clear async communication in between — not a seat in an office.

Senior work, senior pace

Every engagement is staffed by experienced engineers making real architecture and product decisions, not junior hands learning on a client's budget. That means more ownership, and more accountability, for everyone on the team.

What we look for

The qualities that matter more than a resume line

/ 01

Craftsmanship over shortcuts

You sweat the details other people skip — code review, edge cases, accessibility — because you know it compounds over a product's life.

/ 02

Clear, honest communication

You can explain a technical trade-off to a non-technical founder, flag a risk before it becomes a problem, and say "I don't know yet" without spin.

/ 03

Ownership beyond the ticket

You treat client products like your own — noticing what's off, suggesting improvements, and caring about outcomes rather than just closing tasks.

/ 04

Comfortable with modern, boring-on-purpose tools

TypeScript, proven frameworks, well-documented infrastructure — you'd rather ship something maintainable than something merely impressive in a demo.
