FAQ

Answers to what teams usually ask us.

The questions that come up in almost every first conversation — pricing, timelines, technology, and how we actually work together. Don't see yours? Just ask.

It depends on the engagement model. Fixed-scope projects get a fixed price after discovery, once requirements are clear. Dedicated-team and retainer engagements are billed monthly against an agreed allocation. We always scope and quote before any work starts — no open-ended hourly billing with no ceiling in sight.

Timelines vary a lot with scope — a focused web app might ship in 6–10 weeks, a multi-tenant SaaS platform can run several months. We scope in short, working-software cycles rather than one long build, so you're seeing real progress from week one instead of waiting for a single big reveal.

Both. Early-stage founders get help scoping a lean first version without over-building; established teams get a senior partner for a specific product, migration, or overflow of work. What matters more than company size is having a clear owner on your side who can make decisions during the project.

We don't disappear at go-live. Every project transitions into a maintenance or support arrangement if you want one — monitoring, security patches, bug fixes, and small iterations — with the same engineers who built it, not a handoff to a new team who has to relearn the codebase.

We favor proven, well-documented tools over the newest hype: TypeScript, React/Next.js, Node.js and Python on the backend, React Native/Swift/Kotlin for mobile, and managed cloud infrastructure (AWS or GCP). See our full stack for the complete list — the guiding principle is maintainability in three years, not what's trending this week.

Yes — this is one of our more common engagements. We slot into your existing repositories, sprint rituals, and tooling rather than asking you to adopt ours, and we're explicit up front about where our scope starts and ends so there's no ambiguity about ownership.

Weekly demos of working software, a shared backlog you can see at any time, and a running changelog of what shipped and what's next. No status decks assembled the night before a call — the board is always the source of truth, and we reply to inquiries within one business day.

Requirements evolve — that's normal, not a failure of planning. On fixed-scope work we'll walk through the impact on price and timeline together before anything changes. Dedicated-team and retainer engagements are built for exactly this: reprioritizing the backlog is a normal weekly conversation, not a formal change order.
